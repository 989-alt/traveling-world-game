Mission activity loop for a children's world-travel board game played on Chromebooks in a classroom; it plays only during a 30 to 45 second puzzle mission (connect the pairs, place the blocks, order the steps, pick the photo, read the graph). Genre: bright casual-game puzzle groove, sibling of a 112 BPM folk-adventure board theme but a little faster and more playful. Tempo: 124 BPM, 4/4. Instruments: marimba and xylophone lead, ukulele strums, pizzicato strings, soft woodblock and shaker rhythm, light hand claps on beat 2 and 4, a round warm bass; a short glockenspiel question-and-answer phrase every 8 bars like a riddle. Mood: curious, focused, gently hurried, encouraging and never tense or scary; no strong hits or stingers that would clash with short success and failure sound effects. Dynamics stay even so a 20 second countdown ticking can sit on top. About 60 seconds, loops seamlessly with no intro and no ending. Instrumental only, no vocals.
